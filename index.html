Character and individual mutation bonuses are separate.
The character bonus comes from the local player’s Genetics talents. A Fierce pet’s individual bonus comes from its native Bloodline growth curve at the last refreshed level; other known Bloodlines contribute 0%. Use Manual Refresh after changing talents or after a Fierce pet levels up.
